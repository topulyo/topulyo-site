about

Composer · Cellist
Tokyo — New York — Los Angeles

Topu Lyo is a Tokyo-based composer, cellist, producer, and sound artist. His credits include Netflix’s jeen-yuhs: A Kanye Trilogy, Soul Patrol (2026 Sundance Film Festival), and commercial projects for BMW, Volvo, and UNICEF.

He is co-founder of Live Footage, a duo with Mike Thies, and collaborates on Random Together, a hardware-based musical instrument project. His solo album Quiet Scenes From Tokyo is forthcoming on Jan Jelinek’s Faitiche label on November 6, 2026.
