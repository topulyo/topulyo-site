Cello Lessons in Yoyogi Uehara

Learn cello. Explore music. Build confidence in English.

I’m Topu Lyo, a Tokyo-based cellist, composer, and sound artist. I’ve taught cello for over 15 years—including eight years at Crestwood School in New York—and have worked across live performance, recording, and music for film and television. With my duo Live Footage, I’ve composed music for projects including Netflix’s jeen-yuhs, as well as work for HBO and BET.

At Cello & Sound Studio in Yoyogi Uehara, I offer private cello lessons in English for children, teenagers, and adults—from complete beginners to experienced players.

My approach combines a strong technical foundation with curiosity, creativity, and individual expression. Whether you’re learning your first notes, developing your musicianship, or looking for a new way to explore music, lessons are shaped around your interests and goals.

A Different Way to Learn Music

Learning an instrument is about more than technique. It’s an opportunity to develop concentration, confidence, listening, patience, and a deeper relationship with sound.

We’ll work on the fundamentals of playing while making room for experimentation and discovery. Alongside traditional cello repertoire, students can explore improvisation, composition, and creative ways of making music. My goal is to help each student develop the skills and confidence to become an independent musician at their own pace.

Lessons Tailored to You

  • For Children: Lessons build a foundation in technique, rhythm, listening, and musical expression while encouraging curiosity and enjoyment.
  • For Teenagers: Lessons combine technical development and repertoire with improvisation, creative exploration, and a growing sense of artistic identity.
  • For Adults: Lessons offer a supportive space to begin learning, reconnect with an instrument, or deepen an existing musical practice.

No previous musical experience is necessary.

Learn Music in English

All lessons are taught in English.

  • International Families & Expats: The studio offers a welcoming, familiar musical environment in English.
  • Japanese Families: Lessons provide an authentic opportunity for children to encounter and use English naturally through music, conversation, and creative exploration.

Students do not need to be fluent in English to get started. Communication and instruction adapt to each student’s comfort level, allowing them to develop musically while naturally engaging with the language.

What We Can Work On

  • Technique & Fundamentals: Posture, bowing, intonation, tone production, coordination, rhythm, and effective practice habits.
  • Repertoire & Musicianship: Developing musical understanding, interpretation, phrasing, and confidence through pieces suited to your level.
  • Improvisation & Creativity: Exploring sound, experimenting with musical ideas, and developing the ability to listen and respond in the moment.
  • Composition & Recording: For interested students, we can introduce original songwriting, arranging, and basic recording concepts.
